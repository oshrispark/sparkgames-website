Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SparkGames LTD (“SparkGames”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information in connection with SNL, our related website, and communications with us (together, the “Services”).

The information involved depends on the features available in the version you use, your choices, and your device settings. A description of an optional feature does not mean it is available in every version or that all listed information is collected from every player.

Information we collect · How we use information · Sharing · Your choices and rights · Contact

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you create or use an account, contact support, or otherwise communicate with us, we may receive your player name, player identifier, email address, and the information you include in your messages. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for your request.

Sign-in services and guest play

Where sign-in with Apple or Facebook is available and you choose to use it, we may receive an account identifier and profile information made available by that service, such as your name, email address, or profile picture, depending on the permissions you grant. The sign-in provider also processes information under its own privacy policy.

When you play as a guest, information may be associated with a guest or installation identifier rather than a social sign-in account. Guest play does not necessarily mean that no personal information is processed.

Gameplay, device information, and analytics

We may collect information about game sessions, progress, actions within the game, and use of features. Technical information may include your IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, and game or installation identifiers. We use gameplay and technical information to operate the game, understand how features are used, and improve the experience.

Crash reports and diagnostics

We may collect crash reports, error logs, performance measurements, and related device or app information to investigate problems and improve stability. These reports may include identifiers used to connect a technical problem with a game session or installation.

Purchases

Where in-app purchases are available, the app marketplace processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive purchase information, such as the product purchased, transaction identifier, purchase time, and purchase status, to provide purchased content, validate transactions, handle support, and prevent fraud.

Advertising, where available

If the version you use includes advertising, advertising partners may process information such as advertising or other device identifiers, IP address, device and app information, and ad views or interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where personalized advertising is offered, additional disclosures and choices will be provided as required by applicable law and platform rules. Advertising-related processing depends on the advertising features enabled and your applicable choices.

Website visits

Our website hosting providers may process technical request information, including IP address, requested page, browser information, and time of access, to deliver and secure the website. Any additional website tracking will be disclosed with the choices required for that tracking.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the game, manage player sessions, and maintain progress.
  • Enable optional sign-in and purchases where those features are available.
  • Understand gameplay, evaluate features, and improve performance and stability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
  • Protect players and the Services, investigate abuse, and prevent fraud.
  • Deliver and measure advertising where enabled and subject to applicable choices.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

3. How we share information

We may disclose information to the following categories of recipients for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Service providers: providers of hosting, infrastructure, analytics, crash reporting, technical support, and related operational services.
  • Sign-in and marketplace providers: services involved in authentication or in-app purchases when you use those features.
  • Advertising and measurement partners: where advertising is enabled, partners involved in ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable requirements and choices.
  • Legal and safety recipients: authorities, professional advisers, or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or the security of the Services.
  • Business transfer recipients: parties involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of our business, subject to applicable privacy obligations.

Third-party platforms may also collect information directly when you interact with them. Their own notices explain their independent processing. You can contact us for information about the providers relevant to your use of SNL.

4. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Relevant factors include whether an account remains in use, the need to provide support or investigate technical issues, the sensitivity of the information, and applicable legal requirements.

Some information may need to be retained after an account is deleted for accounting, fraud prevention, security, or legal claims. Such retention is limited to the information and period needed for those purposes. Backup copies may remain until removed through the backup lifecycle, with access restricted in the meantime.

5. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed, including encrypted transmission of personal information and controls intended to limit unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

Contact developer@sparkgames.com to make a request. Please identify SNL and provide enough information to locate the relevant account or data. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Do not send passwords or complete payment card information. We handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

Where available, you can also manage permissions and advertising preferences through your device, the game, or your sign-in provider. Disabling a permission or disconnecting a sign-in provider does not necessarily delete information already held by us.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your SNL account and associated personal information by emailing developer@sparkgames.com with the subject “SNL account deletion request”. Include your player identifier if available. You can use this contact method without reinstalling the game.

Following verification, we delete the account and associated personal information, except information retained for the limited reasons described in the Retention section. Deleting the app from your device alone does not necessarily delete account or server-held information. Account deletion does not itself cancel subscriptions managed by an app marketplace.

7. International processing and regional rights

SNL is intended for worldwide availability. Information may be processed outside your country by us or the providers supporting the Services. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we use the safeguards required for those transfers.

For individuals in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions requiring a legal basis, processing may be based on providing the services you request, legitimate interests such as improving and securing the Services, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. The relevant basis depends on the particular processing activity.

Some jurisdictions provide additional rights concerning targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined locally. Where applicable, we provide the disclosures and mechanisms required for the processing we undertake. You can also contact us to exercise applicable rights, including requesting review of a denied request where the law provides that right.

8. Children and younger players

We do not seek to collect personal information from children aged 12 or younger, and we do not knowingly do so. If you believe that a child in this age group has provided personal information to us, please contact developer@sparkgames.com so we can investigate and remove that information.

Players aged 13 to 17 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian to use the Services and must ask that adult to review this Privacy Policy. Where the local age of majority is higher than 18, this requirement continues until the player reaches that age. When we become aware that a player is below the applicable age of majority, we adjust our handling of their information as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Services or data practices change. We will identify the effective date of a published revision and provide additional notice or seek consent where required.
